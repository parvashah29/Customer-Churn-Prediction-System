About Customer Churn

What is Customer Churn?

Customer churn occurs when customers stop using a company's products or services. In the telecom industry, this could mean customers switching to a different provider or canceling their service.

Why Predict Churn?

Predicting customer churn helps businesses:

  • Identify at-risk customers early
  • Take proactive measures to retain customers
  • Improve customer satisfaction
  • Optimize business strategies
